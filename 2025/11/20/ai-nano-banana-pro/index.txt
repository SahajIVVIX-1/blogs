<p>Google DeepMind released <strong>Nano Banana Pro (Gemini 3 Pro Image)</strong> in paid preview through the Gemini API, Google AI Studio and Vertex AI. It follows Nano Banana (Gemini 2.5 Flash Image) from a few months earlier.</p>
<h2 id="the-technical-details">The technical details</h2>
<p>From the post:</p>
<ul>
<li>built on <strong>Gemini 3 Pro</strong>, for higher-fidelity generation and editing</li>
<li>more accurate <strong>text rendering</strong> in images</li>
<li><strong>grounding with Google Search</strong>, to retrieve facts relevant to the prompt</li>
<li>control over <strong>lighting, camera, focus and colour grading</strong>, and over composition</li>
<li><strong>2K and 4K</strong> output</li>
<li>available to coding agents in <strong>Google Antigravity</strong> (e.g. generating UI mockups), and integrated by Adobe and Figma</li>
</ul>
