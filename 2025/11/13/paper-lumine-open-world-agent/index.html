Paper summary: Lumine: An Open Recipe for Building Generalist Agents in 3D Open Worlds

Lumine is an open recipe for a generalist game-playing agent built on a vision-language model: pixels in at 5 Hz, keyboard and mouse out at 30 Hz, completing a five-hour storyline and transferring zero-shot to other games.

Paper card
Paper
Lumine: An Open Recipe for Building Generalist Agents in 3D Open Worlds (2025)
Authors
Weihao Tan, Xiangyang Li, Yunhao Fang, Heyuan Yao, Shi Yan, Hao Luo et al.
Task
Generalist agents for hours-long missions in 3D open worlds
Method
End-to-end VLM-powered agent; 5 Hz pixel perception, 30 Hz keyboard-mouse actions; adaptive reasoning
Data
Trained in Genshin Impact; tested zero-shot in Wuthering Waves and Honkai: Star Rail
Result
Completes the five-hour Mondstadt main storyline at human-level efficiency; zero-shot 100-minute and five-hour missions in other games, per the abstract
Links
arXiv · PDF

Problem

Agents that act in rich 3D worlds for hours, in real time, following natural-language instructions, are far beyond most benchmark agents, which work in short, simplified tasks.

Method

Lumine is presented as the first open recipe for such agents. It uses a human-like interaction loop powered by a vision-language model:

  • perceives raw pixels at 5 Hz
  • outputs precise keyboard and mouse actions at 30 Hz
  • invokes reasoning only when needed, to stay real-time

It’s trained in Genshin Impact.

Key results

From the abstract:

  • completes the entire five-hour Mondstadt main storyline with human-level efficiency
  • follows instructions across exploration, 2D GUI manipulation, collection, combat, puzzle-solving and NPC interaction
  • zero-shot to other games: 100-minute missions in Wuthering Waves and the full five-hour first chapter of Honkai: Star Rail

Limitations

There’s no dedicated limitations section in the extracted text. The demonstrations are in a family of commercial games with similar interaction styles.
