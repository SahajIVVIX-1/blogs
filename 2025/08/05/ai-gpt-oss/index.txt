1e:T50b,<h2 id="what-happened">What happened</h2>
<p><strong>OpenAI</strong> released <strong>GPT OSS</strong>, open-weight models designed for reasoning, agentic tasks and developer use, with day-one Hugging Face support (Transformers with Flash Attention 3, llama.cpp, vLLM, <code>transformers serve</code> and cloud partners).</p>
<h2 id="the-technical-details">The technical details</h2>
<p>From the Hugging Face post:</p>
<ul>
<li>two models: <strong>gpt-oss-120b</strong> (117B parameters) and <strong>gpt-oss-20b</strong> (21B)</li>
<li>both <strong>mixture-of-experts</strong>, with few active parameters per token (the post's overview lists 5.1B and 3.6B)</li>
<li>weights use a <strong>4-bit MXFP4</strong> quantisation scheme</li>
<li>the large model <strong>fits on a single H100</strong>, and the small one <strong>runs in 16GB</strong></li>
<li>licensed <strong>Apache 2.0</strong> with a minimal usage policy</li>
<li>support for system and developer messages, and tool use</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="why-it-matters">Why it matters</h2>
<p>The post quotes OpenAI framing this as a meaningful step in its commitment to the open-source ecosystem.
