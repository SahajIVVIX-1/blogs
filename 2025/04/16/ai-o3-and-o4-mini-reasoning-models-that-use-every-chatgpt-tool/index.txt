24:Tc00,<h2 id="what-happened">What happened</h2>
<p>On 16 April 2025 <strong>OpenAI</strong> released <strong>o3</strong> and <strong>o4-mini</strong>, the latest in its o-series of models trained to think for longer before responding, describing them as its smartest models to date.</p>
<h2 id="the-technical-details">The technical details</h2>
<p>From the OpenAI post:</p>
<ul>
<li>for the first time, reasoning models can <strong>agentically use and combine every tool in ChatGPT</strong>: web search, analyzing uploaded files and data with Python, reasoning about visual inputs, and generating images</li>
<li>trained to reason about <strong>when and how to use tools</strong> to produce answers in the right format, typically in under a minute</li>
<li><strong>update, 10 Jun 2025:</strong> <strong>o3-pro</strong>, a version of o3 designed to think longer for the most reliable answers, is available to Pro users and in the API</li>
<li>the page now notes that o3 and o4-mini are no longer available in ChatGPT and are deprecated in the API</li>
</ul>
