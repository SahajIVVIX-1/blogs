Paper summary: Advances and Challenges in Foundation Agents: From Brain-Inspired Intelligence to Evolutionary, Collaborative, and Safe Systems

A book-length survey that frames LLM agents through brain-inspired modules (memory, world model, reward, goals, emotion), then covers self-improvement, multi-agent systems and agent safety.

Problem

LLM-based agents now reason, perceive and act across many domains, but designing, evaluating and improving them raises many interconnected challenges.

Method

This book-length survey is organised in four parts:

  1. Modular foundations: cognitive, perceptual and operational modules mapped onto analogous human brain functions, covering memory, world modelling, reward processing, goals and emotion.
  2. Self-enhancement and adaptive evolution: how agents refine themselves, adapt, and learn continually through automated optimisation.
  3. Multi-agent systems: collective intelligence from interaction, cooperation and social structures.
  4. Safe and beneficial AI: intrinsic and extrinsic security threats, ethical alignment, robustness and mitigation.

Key results

The contribution is the synthesis and the research agenda it identifies. A companion reading list is maintained on GitHub.

Limitations

As a survey, it reflects the field up to early 2025, and the brain analogies are a framing device rather than empirical claims.
