<p>Google released <strong>Gemini 3 Flash</strong>, expanding the Gemini 3 family a month after Gemini 3 Pro and Gemini 3 Deep Think.</p>

<p>From the post:</p>
<ul>
<li>described as <strong>frontier intelligence built for speed</strong>, at a fraction of the cost of larger models</li>
<li>available in the <strong>Gemini app</strong> and <strong>AI Mode in Search</strong>, where it becomes the default model</li>
<li>for developers: Gemini API in Google AI Studio, Google Antigravity, Gemini CLI, Android Studio, Vertex AI and Gemini Enterprise</li>
<li>Google says that since Gemini 3's launch, its API had been processing <strong>over 1T tokens per day</strong></li>
</ul>
