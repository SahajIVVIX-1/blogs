22:T4d4,<h2 id="what-happened">What happened</h2>
<p><strong>vLLM v0.9.0</strong> was released, with <strong>649 commits</strong> from <strong>215 contributors</strong> (82 new).</p>
<h2 id="the-technical-details">The technical details</h2>
<p>From the release notes:</p>
<ul>
<li>upgrade to <strong>PyTorch 2.7</strong>, and the default wheel moves from <strong>CUDA 12.4 to 12.8</strong></li>
<li>an initial set of <strong>optimised NVIDIA Blackwell kernels</strong> for attention and MLP, plus FlashInfer support</li>
<li>initial support for large-scale inference:
<ul>
<li><strong>EP</strong> (expert parallelism): MoE permute/unpermute kernels and an all-to-all interface</li>
<li><strong>DP</strong> (data parallelism): engine process management decoupled from communication</li>
<li><strong>PD</strong> (prefill/decode disaggregation): NIXL integration and multiple KV connectors</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>documentation moved from Sphinx to MkDocs</li>
</ul>
