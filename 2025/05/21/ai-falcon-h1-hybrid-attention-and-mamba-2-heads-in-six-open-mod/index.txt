24:Tc3b,<h2 id="what-happened">What happened</h2>
<p>On 21 May 2025 the Technology Innovation Institute introduced the <strong>Falcon-H1</strong> series through <strong>Hugging Face</strong>: six open-weight models in 0.5B, 1.5B, 1.5B-Deep, 3B, 7B and 34B sizes, each with base and instruction-tuned variants.</p>
<h2 id="the-technical-details">The technical details</h2>
<p>From the Hugging Face post:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>hybrid mixer block:</strong> classical <strong>Transformer attention</strong> combined with <strong>Mamba-2</strong> state-space heads <strong>in parallel</strong>, with the number of each adjustable independently</li>
<li>claimed benefits: <strong>faster inference, lower memory usage</strong> and strong generalization across tasks</li>
<li>performance is said to rival top Transformer-based models in each size tier</li>
<li><strong>multilingual by design</strong>: 18 languages supported natively</li>
<li>released under a permissive license based on <strong>Apache 2.0</strong></li>
</ul>
