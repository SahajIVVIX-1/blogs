Paper summary

Paper summary: SmolLM2: When Smol Goes Big -- Data-Centric Training of a Small Language Model

SmolLM2 is a 1.7B model "overtrained" on about 11 trillion tokens in stages that mix web, maths, code and instruction data, with new datasets (FineMath, Stack-Edu, SmolTalk) built where existing ones were too small or noisy.

  • Training a small model on far more tokens than compute-optimal ("overtraining") pays off when the model must be cheap to run.
  • Mixing rates are adjusted stage by stage based on how the previous stage performed.

Deep Learning · Optimisers: SGD, momentum, Adam

Mini-batch SGD, momentum, RMSProp-style scaling and Adam compared on an ill-conditioned valley, using torch.optim.

  • Momentum smooths noisy gradients and speeds up travel along long, shallow valleys.
  • Adam adapts a separate step size per parameter from running averages of the gradient and its square.
