1b:T4cc,<h2 id="what-happened">What happened</h2>
<p>OpenAI released <strong>Deep Research</strong>, a system that browses the web and answers questions from what it reads. Hugging Face's post the next day describes a <strong>24-hour effort to reproduce it openly</strong>.</p>
<h2 id="the-technical-details">The technical details</h2>
<p>From the post:</p>
<ul>
<li>OpenAI reported large gains on <strong>GAIA</strong>: near <strong>67%</strong> correct on average (1-shot), and <strong>47.6%</strong> on the hardest "level 3" questions</li>
<li>Deep Research combines an LLM with an <strong>agentic framework</strong> that guides tool use (like web search) in steps, and OpenAI disclosed little about that framework</li>
<li>the open version uses a <strong>CodeAgent</strong> (from smolagents, the previous post) plus custom browsing and text-inspection tools</li>
<li>the post includes results, community reproductions and next steps</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="why-it-matters">Why it matters</h2>
<p>It shows that much of an agent's power is in the <strong>framework around the model</strong> (tools, loop, memory), not only the model.
