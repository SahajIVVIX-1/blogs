Paper summary

Paper summary: MiniMax-M1: Scaling Test-Time Compute Efficiently with Lightning Attention

MiniMax-M1 is an open-weight hybrid-attention reasoning model (456B total, 45.9B active, 1M-token context) trained with large-scale RL and a new algorithm, CISPO, at a reported rental cost of $534,700.

  • Lightning attention keeps long reasoning traces cheap, which matters when a model "thinks" for tens of thousands of tokens.
  • CISPO clips importance-sampling weights instead of clipping token updates.
