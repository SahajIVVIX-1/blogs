Paper summary: LongRoPE: Extending LLM Context Window Beyond 2 Million Tokens

LongRoPE extends a pretrained LLM's context window to 2048k tokens by searching for non-uniform RoPE interpolation, extending progressively, and readjusting for short contexts.

  • Positional interpolation works better when it isn't uniform across RoPE dimensions and token positions.
  • Extension happens in stages, 256k with fine-tuning then a second interpolation to 2048k, with only up to 1k fine-tuning steps.
