Paper summary: SAM 2: Segment Anything in Images and Videos

SAM 2 extends Segment Anything to video with a streaming-memory Transformer and a data engine that built the largest video segmentation dataset to date.

Paper card
Paper
SAM 2: Segment Anything in Images and Videos (2024)
Authors
Nikhila Ravi, Valentin Gabeur, Yuan-Ting Hu, Ronghang Hu, Chaitanya Ryali, Tengyu Ma et al.
Task
Promptable segmentation in images and videos
Method
Transformer with streaming memory; data engine that improves model and data through user interaction
Data
Largest video segmentation dataset to date, collected with the data engine
Result
Better video accuracy with 3× fewer interactions; more accurate and 6× faster than SAM on images, per the abstract
Links
arXiv · PDF

Problem

The original Segment Anything Model (SAM) segments any object in a single image from a click or box. Video adds time: objects move, get occluded and reappear, and you want to prompt once and have the mask follow the object.

Method

  • Model: a simple Transformer with a streaming memory. Frames are processed one at a time, and the memory carries information about the target object forward, which enables real-time video processing.
  • Data engine: model and annotators improve each other through interaction. The engine collected the largest video segmentation dataset to date.

Key results

From the abstract: in video segmentation, better accuracy with 3× fewer interactions than prior approaches. In image segmentation, more accurate and 6× faster than SAM. The model, dataset, training code and demo are released.

Limitations

From the paper:

  • may fail across shot changes
  • can lose or confuse objects in crowded scenes, after long occlusions or in long videos (extra clicks on other frames usually recover it)
  • struggles with very thin, fast-moving details and with nearby objects that look alike, such as identical juggling balls; the authors suggest more explicit motion modelling
