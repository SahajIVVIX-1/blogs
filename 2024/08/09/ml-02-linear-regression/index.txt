1f:T3aa94,<h2 id="1-academic--historical-context">1. Academic &#x26; Historical Context</h2>
<p>Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) is the foundational cornerstone of statistical learning and predictive modeling. Formally introduced by <strong>Adrien-Marie Legendre (1805)</strong> in his work <em>Nouvelles méthodes pour la détermination des orbites des comètes</em> and rigorously linked to probability theory by <strong>Carl Friedrich Gauss (1809)</strong> in <em>Theoria Motus Corporum Coelestium</em>, linear regression established the first mathematically closed-form parameter estimation technique for empirical data.</p>
<p>Under the <strong>Gauss-Markov Theorem</strong>, the OLS estimator is proved to be the <strong>BLUE</strong> (<em>Best Linear Unbiased Estimator</em>): among all linear unbiased estimators, OLS achieves the minimum sampling variance, requiring no distributional assumptions on the error term other than zero mean, homoscedasticity, and uncorrelated residuals.</p>

<h2 id="8-summary-checklist-for-engineering-practice">8. Summary Checklist for Engineering Practice</h2>
<ul class="contains-task-list">
<li class="task-list-item"><input type="checkbox" checked disabled> <strong>Subspace Perspective</strong>: Recognize that fitting OLS is geometrically finding the shadow of target <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi mathvariant="bold">y</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\mathbf{y}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6389em;vertical-align:-0.1944em;"></span><span class="mord mathbf" style="margin-right:0.016em;">y</span></span></span></span> cast on the hyper-plane spanned by the feature columns.</li>
<li class="task-list-item"><input type="checkbox" checked disabled> <strong>Inversion Caution</strong>: Never compute <code>inv(X.T @ X)</code> in numerical code. Prefer SVD (<code>np.linalg.lstsq</code> / LAPACK <code>dgelsd</code>) or QR decomposition.</li>
<li class="task-list-item"><input type="checkbox" checked disabled> <strong>Collinearity Audit</strong>: Check condition numbers and VIF before interpreting regression coefficients as causal feature importances.</li>
<li class="task-list-item"><input type="checkbox" checked disabled> <strong>Residual Inspection</strong>: Always plot residuals against fitted values to detect non-linear curvature or heteroscedastic fan shapes.</li>
