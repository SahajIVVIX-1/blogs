Paper summary: xGen-MM (BLIP-3): A Family of Open Large Multimodal Models

BLIP-3 (xGen-MM) is Salesforce's open framework for large multimodal models (datasets, recipe, architecture and models), releasing 4B and 14B base and instruction-tuned models that handle interleaved image–text input.

  • The release is the whole recipe: curated datasets, training method and models, not just weights.
  • The models can take interleaved images and text, including multi-image inputs.
