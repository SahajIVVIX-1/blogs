Paper summary

Paper summary: Differential Transformer

Differential attention computes attention as the difference between two softmax maps, cancelling common-mode noise; Diff Transformer is reported to beat Transformers on scaling, long context, retrieval and hallucination.

  • Subtracting one attention map from another cancels attention that both give to irrelevant tokens.
  • The paper also reports fewer activation outliers, which matters for quantisation.

Machine Learning · Boosting and XGBoost

Boosting builds shallow trees one after another, each fixing the previous errors; gradient boosting, learning rate, early stopping, and XGBoost.

  • Bagging averages independent trees; boosting adds trees sequentially, each fitting what is still wrong.
  • A small learning rate with early stopping is the standard way to keep boosting from overfitting.
