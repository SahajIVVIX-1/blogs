24:T15b8,<h2 id="what-happened">What happened</h2>
<p>On 1 October 2024 <strong>OpenAI</strong> introduced a public beta of the <strong>Realtime API</strong>, letting all paid developers add low-latency voice conversations to their apps. The same day it announced audio input and output in the <strong>Chat Completions API</strong> for use cases that do not need the lowest latency.</p>
