24:Tc9e,<h2 id="what-happened">What happened</h2>
<p>On 13 May 2024 <strong>OpenAI</strong> announced <strong>GPT-4o</strong> (the “o” is for “omni”), a step toward more natural human-computer interaction. It accepts text, audio, image and video as input and generates text, audio and image outputs.</p>
<h2 id="the-technical-details">The technical details</h2>
<p>From the OpenAI post:</p>
<ul>
<li>responds to audio in <strong>232 ms at best and 320 ms on average</strong>, similar to human response time in conversation</li>
<li>matches <strong>GPT-4 Turbo</strong> on English text and code, with significant gains on non-English text, while being <strong>much faster and 50% cheaper</strong> in the API</li>
<li>especially stronger at <strong>vision and audio</strong> understanding than earlier models</li>
<li>before GPT-4o, Voice Mode used three models (transcribe, GPT-3.5 or GPT-4, text-to-speech) with average latencies of <strong>2.8 s</strong> (GPT-3.5) and <strong>5.4 s</strong> (GPT-4), and the middle model could not see tone, multiple speakers or background noise</li>
<li>GPT-4o is a <strong>single new model trained end-to-end</strong> across text, vision and audio</li>
</ul>
