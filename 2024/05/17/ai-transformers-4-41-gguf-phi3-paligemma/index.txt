21:T4de,<h2 id="what-happened">What happened</h2>
<p><strong>Transformers v4.41.0</strong> added several models and GGUF support.</p>
<h2 id="the-technical-details">The technical details</h2>
<p>From the release notes:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Phi-3</strong> (Microsoft), with new RoPE scaling methods. Phi-3-mini comes in <strong>4K and 128K</strong> context variants, described as the first in its class to support 128K tokens with little impact on quality. The technical report is summarised here on 23 April 2024.</li>
<li><strong>JetMoE-8B</strong>: an MoE model where each block has <strong>two MoE layers</strong>, a mixture of attention heads and a mixture of MLP experts. The notes report training throughput around 100B tokens per day on 96 H100s.</li>
<li><strong>PaliGemma</strong>: a lightweight open VLM inspired by PaLI-3, built from open components such as the <strong>SigLIP</strong> vision model.</li>
<li><strong>GGUF</strong> loading support, plus VideoLlava, Falcon2 and FalconVLM.</li>
</ul>
