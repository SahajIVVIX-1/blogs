23:Tb3b,<h2 id="what-happened">What happened</h2>
<p><strong>vLLM v0.4.0</strong>, an open-source LLM serving engine, was released.</p>
<h2 id="the-technical-details">The technical details</h2>
<p>From the release notes:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Automatic prefix caching</strong>: long shared prefixes, such as a system prompt, are cached across requests. Enable it with <code>--enable-prefix-caching</code>.</li>
<li>OpenAI-compatible server: <code>json_object</code> output, a <code>--use-delay</code> flag to improve time-to-first-token under load, and <code>min_tokens</code>.</li>
<li>progress on a <strong>chunked prefill</strong> scheduler and <strong>speculative decoding</strong></li>
<li>new models: Command R+, Qwen2 MoE, DBRX, XVerse, Jais, and the vision-language model <strong>LLaVA</strong></li>
<li>better AWS Inferentia (Neuron) support and a CMake-based build</li>
</ul>
