Paper summary: The Unreasonable Ineffectiveness of the Deeper Layers

Removing up to about half of the layers of some open LLMs, chosen by layer similarity and followed by a little QLoRA "healing", barely hurts question-answering performance.

  • Choosing which contiguous block of layers to drop by representation similarity, then healing with QLoRA, leaves QA performance largely intact.
  • Either pre-training underuses the deeper layers, or the shallow layers do most of the knowledge storage.
