Paper summary: Self-Rewarding Language Models

A model judges its own responses with LLM-as-a-Judge prompting and trains on those rewards with iterative DPO, so both its instruction following and its judging improve over three rounds.

  • The same model generates candidates, scores them as a judge, and learns from the resulting preference pairs.
  • The authors report that the model's ability to reward itself also improves across iterations.
