1d:T560,<h2 id="what-happened">What happened</h2>
<p><strong>PyTorch 2.2</strong> was released, with 3,628 commits from 521 contributors since 2.1.</p>
<h2 id="the-technical-details">The technical details</h2>
<p>From the release notes:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong><code>scaled_dot_product_attention</code> (SDPA)</strong> now supports <strong>FlashAttention-2</strong>, with about <strong>2× speedups</strong> over previous versions</li>
<li><strong>AOTInductor</strong>: an ahead-of-time extension of TorchInductor that compiles and deploys PyTorch programs for <strong>non-Python, server-side</strong> environments</li>
<li><strong><code>device_mesh</code></strong>: a new abstraction in <code>torch.distributed</code> for initialising and representing process groups</li>
<li><strong><code>TORCH_LOGS</code></strong>: a standard, configurable logging mechanism</li>
<li>better <code>torch.compile</code> support for <strong>optimizers</strong>, plus Inductor fusion and layout improvements</li>
<li>PyTorch 2.2.x is the <strong>last release supporting macOS x86</strong></li>
</ul>
