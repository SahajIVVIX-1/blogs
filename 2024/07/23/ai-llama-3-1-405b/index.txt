1d:T521,<h2 id="what-happened">What happened</h2>
<p><strong>Meta</strong> released <strong>Llama 3.1</strong>: eight open-weight models on the Hub (3 base, 5 fine-tuned), with Hugging Face integration across Transformers, TGI, HuggingChat (405B instruct) and several clouds.</p>
<h2 id="the-technical-details">The technical details</h2>
<p>From the Hugging Face post:</p>
<ul>
<li>three sizes: <strong>8B</strong> (consumer GPU), <strong>70B</strong> (large-scale apps) and <strong>405B</strong> (for <strong>synthetic data</strong>, <strong>LLM-as-a-judge</strong> or <strong>distillation</strong>)</li>
<li>multilingual, with long-context support</li>
<li><strong>built-in and custom tool calling</strong> in the prompt format</li>
<li>safety models: <strong>Llama Guard 3</strong> (classifies inputs and outputs) and <strong>Prompt Guard</strong> (detects prompt injections and jailbreaks)</li>
<li>quantised <strong>FP8, AWQ and GPTQ</strong> versions for easier inference, plus memory-requirement tables for inference and training</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="why-it-matters">Why it matters</h2>
<p>A 405B open-weight model changed what "open" could mean at the top end, mainly as a <strong>teacher</strong> for smaller models.
