Matplotlib · Line, bar, scatter and histogram

The four plots I'll use most and when each fits: line for trends, bar for categories, scatter for relationships, histogram for distributions.

  • Pick the chart from the question: trend gives line, comparison gives bar, relationship gives scatter, distribution gives histogram.
  • The number of histogram bins changes the story, so try a few.
