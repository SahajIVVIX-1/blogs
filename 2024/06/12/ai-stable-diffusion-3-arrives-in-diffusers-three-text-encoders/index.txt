24:Tbe4,<h2 id="what-happened">What happened</h2>
<p>On 12 June 2024 <strong>Hugging Face</strong> announced that <strong>Stable Diffusion 3</strong> (Stability AI's latest Stable Diffusion model) is available on the Hub and usable with <strong>Diffusers</strong>. The model released that day is <strong>Stable Diffusion 3 Medium</strong>, with <strong>2B parameters</strong>.</p>
<h2 id="the-technical-details">The technical details</h2>
<p>From the Hugging Face post:</p>
<ul>
<li>a <strong>latent diffusion</strong> model with <strong>three text encoders</strong> (CLIP L/14, OpenCLIP bigG/14 and T5-v1.1-XXL), a new <strong>Multimodal Diffusion Transformer (MMDiT)</strong> and a <strong>16-channel autoencoder</strong> similar to SDXL's</li>
<li>text and pixel latents are processed as a sequence of embeddings; <strong>MMDiT blocks use two separate sets of weights</strong> to embed text and image sequences, then join them for attention</li>
<li>Diffusers support includes <strong>Dreambooth and LoRA training scripts</strong>, plus <strong>memory optimizations</strong> to run on a range of hardware and <strong>performance optimizations</strong></li>
</ul>
