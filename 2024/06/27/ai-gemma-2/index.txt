22:T4cf,<h2 id="what-happened">What happened</h2>
<p><strong>Google</strong> released <strong>Gemma 2</strong>, four open-weight models, with Hugging Face integration (Transformers v4.42, Google Cloud, Inference Endpoints).</p>
<h2 id="the-technical-details">The technical details</h2>
<p>From the Hugging Face post:</p>
<ul>
<li>sizes <strong>9B</strong> and <strong>27B</strong>, each <strong>base</strong> and <strong>instruction-tuned</strong>, with an <strong>8K</strong> context</li>
<li>trained on about <strong>2× the data</strong> of Gemma 1: <strong>13T tokens</strong> for 27B and 8T for 9B</li>
<li>technical advances the post lists:
<ul>
<li><strong>sliding window attention</strong> (interleaved with full attention)</li>
<li><strong>logit soft-capping</strong></li>
<li><strong>knowledge distillation</strong> (smaller models learning from a larger teacher)</li>
<li><strong>model merging</strong></li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="why-it-matters">Why it matters</h2>
<p>Each technique is a cost/quality lever. Sliding windows cut attention cost on long inputs, and distillation transfers ability to smaller models.
