1f:T467,<h2 id="what-happened">What happened</h2>
<p><strong>Meta</strong> released <strong>Llama 3.2</strong>: ten open-weight models (5 multimodal and 5 text-only), integrated by Hugging Face in Transformers and TGI with deployment options.</p>
<h2 id="the-technical-details">The technical details</h2>
<p>From the Hugging Face post:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Llama 3.2 Vision</strong>: <strong>11B</strong> (consumer GPU) and <strong>90B</strong> (large-scale), base and instruction-tuned</li>
<li><strong>small text models</strong>: <strong>1B</strong> and <strong>3B</strong>, base and instruct, designed to run <strong>on-device</strong></li>
<li><strong>Llama Guard 3</strong> with <strong>vision</strong> support, plus a small <strong>1B</strong> Llama Guard</li>
<li>the post also has a section on licence changes for the vision models that affect users in the EU</li>
</ul>
