24:Tcd3,<h2 id="what-happened">What happened</h2>
<p>On 21 January 2026 the <strong>PyTorch</strong> team released <strong>2.10.0</strong>.</p>
<h2 id="the-technical-details">The technical details</h2>
<p>From the release notes:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Python 3.14</strong> support for <code>torch.compile()</code>, with the free-threaded <strong>3.14t</strong> build experimental</li>
<li><strong>combo-kernels horizontal fusion</strong> in TorchInductor to reduce kernel-launch overhead</li>
<li>a new <strong><code>varlen_attn()</code></strong> op supporting ragged and packed sequences</li>
<li><strong><code>DebugMode</code></strong> for tracking dispatched calls and debugging numerical divergence, and <code>torch.compile()</code> now respects <code>use_deterministic_mode</code></li>
<li><strong>Intel GPUs:</strong> support for Panther Lake on Windows and Linux with FP8 and complex MatMul</li>
<li>breaking: <strong>ONNX exporter</strong> defaults to <code>dynamo=True</code> without fallback; the <code>pytorch-triton</code> package is renamed <code>triton</code></li>
</ul>
