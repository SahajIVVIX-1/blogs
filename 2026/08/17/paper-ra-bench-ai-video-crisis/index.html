Paper summary: Can We Defend Against AI-Generated Video Attacks on Real-World Crisis Events? A Systematic Evaluation of Detectors, Generators and Social Dissemination

RA-Bench pairs 1,830 real crisis videos with 16,056 generated clips from nine generators, and finds that no family of AI-video detectors generalises consistently, including when videos are re-shared on social media.

Paper card
Paper
Can We Defend Against AI-Generated Video Attacks on Real-World Crisis Events? A Systematic Evaluation of Detectors, Generators and Social Dissemination (2026)
Authors
Shuo Liang, Yixing Ma, Pengfei Zhou, Zhenglin Wan, Xingyan Chen, Zihan Mei et al.
Task
Detecting AI-generated videos of real-world crises
Method
RA-Bench with real-video anchors across 10 social-risk categories; evaluation of detectors, generation conditions, human judgements and social dissemination
Data
17,886 videos: 1,830 real anchors + 16,056 clips from 4 open and 5 closed generators
Result
No detector family generalises consistently across the benchmark, per the abstract
Links
arXiv · PDF

Problem

Video generators can now fabricate realistic footage of wars, disasters and emergencies, a serious misinformation risk. Existing benchmarks say little about how detectors behave in exactly these settings.

Method

RA-Bench uses real videos as anchors: 1,830 real videos across 10 social-risk categories, plus 16,056 generated clips from four open-source and five closed-source generators. The evaluation has three parts:

  1. detector generalisation: seven traditional detectors, ten zero-shot multimodal models in three review settings, and two MLLMs fine-tuned for AI-video detection
  2. how detectability changes with generation quality, conditioning and seeds
  3. human authenticity judgements, and detector reliability during social dissemination (re-encoding and re-sharing)

Key results

From the abstract: none of the three detector families generalises consistently. Generation properties affect the families differently, while source-level detection patterns stay stable across seeds.

Limitations

There’s no dedicated limitations section in the extracted text. Generators change quickly, so any snapshot benchmark ages.
