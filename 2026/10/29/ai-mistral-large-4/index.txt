20:T6c3,<h2 id="what-happened">What happened</h2>
<p><strong>Mistral</strong> released <strong>Mistral Large 4</strong>, nicknamed "Le Chonk", with Hugging Face support in Transformers, vLLM and llama.cpp (GGUFs by Unsloth and the llama.cpp team), TRL fine-tuning, and Inference Endpoints. The post was published on 29 October 2026.</p>
<h2 id="the-technical-details">The technical details</h2>
<p>From the Hugging Face post:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>multimodal</strong> (text and images in, text out), <strong>multilingual</strong>, <strong>Mixture-of-Experts</strong></li>
<li>about <strong>1T total parameters</strong>, with about <strong>49B active</strong> according to the post's introduction (the checkpoint names use "A52B")</li>
<li><strong>1M-token</strong> context</li>
<li><strong>two forms of sparsity</strong>: the language model selects a small group of <strong>experts</strong> per token, and an <strong>attention indexer</strong> selects which earlier positions to attend to</li>
<li>a <strong>Pixtral</strong> vision encoder</li>
<li>official <strong>FP8</strong> and <strong>NVFP4</strong> variants</li>
<li>the <strong>Mistral Ecosystem Open-Weight License</strong>: an MIT derivative with limitations for model-as-a-service offerings</li>
<li>the post highlights strength in <strong>cybersecurity</strong>, <strong>coding agents</strong> and multimodality</li>
</ul>
