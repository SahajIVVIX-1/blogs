Built this: Social media sentiment and trend analytics on MongoDB

A Big Data Analytics course project: 2,944,814 real tweets in MongoDB, cleaned and enriched with sentiment, topics and keywords, served by FastAPI to a React dashboard, with Spark and sharding experiments.

  • The README is upfront about the data: real tweets, but all from state-linked troll accounts, with synthetic engagement counts that are clearly flagged.
  • Experiments measure ingestion, indexing, aggregation and NLP at 100K, 500K, 1M and 2.95M documents.
