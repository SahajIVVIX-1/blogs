Paper summary: JoyAI-VL-Interaction: Real-Time Vision-Language Interaction Intelligence

JoyAI-VL-Interaction is an open 8B vision-language model that watches a live video stream and decides each second whether to stay silent, respond, or hand off to a bigger background model.

  • Most assistants only answer when asked; this one watches continuously and decides for itself when to speak.
  • The authors say plainly that much larger products beat it at general chat; they claim an advantage only in real-time, event-driven scenarios.

  • DCGAN's guidelines: strided convolutions instead of pooling, batch norm, ReLU in the generator, LeakyReLU in the discriminator.
