Paper summary: Utonia: Toward One Encoder for All Point Clouds

Utonia trains one self-supervised point-transformer encoder across very different point-cloud domains (remote sensing, LiDAR, indoor RGB-D, CAD, video-lifted points), and its features also help robot policies and spatial reasoning in VLMs.

Paper card
Paper
Utonia: Toward One Encoder for All Point Clouds (2026)
Authors
Yujia Zhang, Xiaoyang Wu, Yunhan Yang, Xianzhe Fan, Han Li, Yuechen Zhang et al.
Task
A single encoder for point clouds from all domains
Method
Self-supervised point transformer trained jointly across domains
Data
Remote sensing, outdoor LiDAR, indoor RGB-D, object CAD models, point clouds lifted from RGB videos
Result
Better perception, emergent cross-domain behaviours, gains for VLA manipulation and VLM spatial reasoning, per the abstract
Links
arXiv · PDF

Problem

Point-cloud models are usually trained per domain: driving LiDAR, indoor scans, CAD objects. Could one model learn from all of them?

Method

Utonia trains a single self-supervised point transformer encoder across remote sensing, outdoor LiDAR, indoor RGB-D sequences, object-centric CAD models, and point clouds lifted from RGB-only videos. These domains differ in sensing geometry, density and priors.

Key results

From the abstract:

  • a consistent representation space that transfers across domains
  • better perception, plus emergent behaviours that only appear with joint training
  • conditioning vision-language-action policies on Utonia features improves robotic manipulation, and adding them to VLMs improves spatial reasoning

Limitations

There’s no dedicated limitations section in the extracted text. The authors present it as a first step toward foundation models for sparse 3D data.
