24:Td5e,<h2 id="what-happened">What happened</h2>
<p>On 31 March 2026 <strong>Hugging Face</strong> released <strong>TRL v1.0.0</strong>, the first major version of its post-training library, with a companion blog post titled “TRL v1: Post-Training Library Built to Move with the Field”.</p>
<h2 id="the-technical-details">The technical details</h2>
<p>From the release notes:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Asynchronous GRPO:</strong> decouples generation from the gradient-update loop by <strong>offloading rollouts to an external vLLM server</strong>, so generation and training run in parallel and hardware is used better</li>
<li><strong>VESPO</strong> (Variational Sequence-Level Soft Policy Optimization): targets instability from <strong>policy staleness, asynchronous updates and train-inference mismatch</strong> with a smooth asymmetric Gamma weighting of extreme sequence-level importance weights, enabled with <code>loss_type="vespo"</code> in <code>GRPOConfig</code></li>
<li><strong>DPPO</strong> (Divergence Proximal Policy Optimization): an experimental trainer replacing PPO clipping with divergence constraints for trust-region updates</li>
<li><strong>SDPO</strong> (Self-Distillation Policy Optimization): an experimental trainer that distills a model's feedback-informed predictions on its own high-reward trajectories back into the policy</li>
</ul>
