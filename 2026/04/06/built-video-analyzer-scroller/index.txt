1f:T5e4,<p>On 6 April 2026 I committed the first version of this suite (22 commits so far). It holds three independent tools, all Python with PyQt6.</p>
<h2 id="the-three-tools">The three tools</h2>
<p>According to the README:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Video Folder Analyzer</strong>: walks folders recursively, reads each video's duration with <strong>FFmpeg</strong>, and exports a formatted Excel report with <code>openpyxl</code>: grouped rows, frozen panes, borders and clickable paths. It runs as a GUI or from the command line.</li>
<li><strong>PDF Scroller Generator</strong>: turns multi-page PDFs into smooth <strong>1080p scrolling MP4s</strong>, using Poppler/<code>pdf2image</code> and <strong>OpenCV</strong>. In tabular mode, it slices off the repeating header and pins it at the top while the rest scrolls. Header bounds are chosen in an interactive preview.</li>
<li><strong>Bhajan Video Generator</strong>: merges several devotional-lyric PDFs into one scrolling video. It detects table borders to stitch pages exactly, finds white strips between blocks to <strong>pause</strong> for reading, auto-fills related PDFs by keyword, and prefers <strong>GPU encoders</strong> (NVENC, QSV, AMF), falling back to <code>libx264</code>.</li>
</ol>
