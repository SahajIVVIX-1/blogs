1f:T566,<p>On 24 April 2026 I pushed <strong>Orbit-Wars</strong>, an agent for the <strong>Kaggle Orbit Wars</strong> environment, written in Python.</p>
<h2 id="how-the-agent-plays">How the agent plays</h2>
<p>From the README:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Economic priority scoring</strong>: rank target planets by production value, distance and current ship count, so high-production planets are taken first.</li>
<li><strong>Threat assessment and "fortress" logic</strong>: find planets that are about to fall and reinforce them from nearby bases.</li>
<li><strong>Fleet sizing</strong>: send a margin above what's needed (the README gives the formula 2.5x + 20) for a likely capture without stripping defences.</li>
<li><strong>Sun bypass</strong>: detect paths that would hit the sun and steer around it.</li>
<li><strong>Predictive intercepts</strong>: compute where an orbiting planet will be at the fleet's arrival time.</li>
<li>It also accounts for fleet speed depending on fleet size, and chases high-value comets.</li>
</ul>
<p><code>test_agent.py</code> runs a match against a random opponent and saves an HTML replay.</p>
