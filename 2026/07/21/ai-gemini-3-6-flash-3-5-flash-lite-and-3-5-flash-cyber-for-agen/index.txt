24:Tc00,<h2 id="what-happened">What happened</h2>
<p>On 21 July 2026 <strong>Google DeepMind</strong> introduced three Gemini models built on Gemini 3.5 Flash, for developers and customers building production agents that need higher token efficiency, lower latency and more reliable performance.</p>
<h2 id="the-technical-details">The technical details</h2>
<p>From the Google DeepMind post:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>3.6 Flash:</strong> the workhorse, with better coding, knowledge work and multimodal performance; <strong>17% fewer output tokens</strong> than 3.5 Flash (Artificial Analysis Index) and up to <strong>65%</strong> on <strong>DeepSWE by Datacurve</strong>, at a lower cost per output token</li>
<li><strong>3.5 Flash-Lite:</strong> the fastest, most cost-effective 3.5-class model, <strong>350 output tokens per second</strong>, with large gains over earlier Flash-Lite generations in agentic workflows</li>
<li><strong>3.5 Flash Cyber in CodeMender:</strong> a specialized, efficient cyber-focused model paired with the <strong>CodeMender</strong> code security agent, positioned as competitive at the frontier</li>
</ul>
