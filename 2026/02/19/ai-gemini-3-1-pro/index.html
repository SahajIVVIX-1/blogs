Gemini 3.1 Pro: upgraded core reasoning

Google released Gemini 3.1 Pro in preview, the core model behind the previous week's Gemini 3 Deep Think update, rolling out across the Gemini API, Antigravity, Vertex AI, the Gemini app and NotebookLM.

What happened
