AI updates

Summaries of releases and announcements from official sources, dated by the source's publish date.

  • 29 Oct 2026 · AI updates

    Mistral Large 4 ("Le Chonk"): ~1T-parameter MoE with sparse attention

    Mistral Large 4 is a multimodal MoE of about 1T total parameters with a 1M-token context, combining expert sparsity with sparse attention and a Pixtral vision encoder, released in FP8 and NVFP4 under an MIT-derived open-weight licence.

  • 28 Sept 2026 · AI updates

    Hugging Face Hub adds RL environments

    The Hugging Face Hub gained a filter for RL environments: an environment is a dataset repo (tasks, tests, containers and a reward rule) with a "Use this dataset" command for frameworks like Harbor, Verifiers and NVIDIA NeMo Gym.

  • 13 Aug 2026 · AI updates

    Gemini 3.7 Flash: three weeks after 3.6

    Google released Gemini 3.7 Flash three weeks after 3.6 Flash, reporting large gains on coding (DeepSWE v1.1 65.3% vs 49.0%), web development and document-heavy knowledge work, at half 3.6 Flash's introductory price.

  • 15 Jul 2026 · AI updates

    Inkling: a ~1T-parameter open multimodal model with 1M context

    Thinking Machines released Inkling on Hugging Face, a ~1T-parameter decoder-only multimodal MoE (41B active) that takes image, text and audio, has a 1M context, and was trained on 45T tokens; a smaller Inkling-Small followed.

  • 3 Jun 2026 · AI updates

    Gemma 4 12B: an encoder-free multimodal model for laptops

    Google introduced Gemma 4 12B, a mid-sized open model where vision and audio inputs flow straight into the LLM backbone with no separate encoders; it runs in 16GB and ships with multi-token-prediction drafters.

  • 19 May 2026 · AI updates

    Gemini 3.5 Flash: agents and coding at Flash speed

    Google DeepMind introduced Gemini 3.5 with 3.5 Flash first, reporting it beats Gemini 3.1 Pro on agentic and coding benchmarks (Terminal-Bench 2.1 76.2%, MCP Atlas 83.6%) at about 4× the output speed of other frontier models.

  • 2 Apr 2026 · AI updates

    Gemma 4: open multimodal models under Apache 2.0

    Google DeepMind's Gemma 4 family is Apache 2.0, accepts images or video plus text (and audio for the small models), and adds Per-Layer Embeddings and a shared KV cache for efficiency.

  • 5 Mar 2026 · AI updates

    Modular Diffusers: diffusion pipelines from composable blocks

    Hugging Face Diffusers introduced ModularPipeline, which builds diffusion pipelines from reusable blocks (text encoding, image encoding, denoising, decoding) that can be inspected, swapped and run on their own.

  • 19 Feb 2026 · AI updates

    Gemini 3.1 Pro: upgraded core reasoning

    Google released Gemini 3.1 Pro in preview, the core model behind the previous week's Gemini 3 Deep Think update, rolling out across the Gemini API, Antigravity, Vertex AI, the Gemini app and NotebookLM.

  • 26 Jan 2026 · AI updates

    Transformers v5: the first major release in five years

    Hugging Face released Transformers v5, the first major version in five years, removing long-deprecated APIs, refactoring weight loading and tokenization, and moving to weekly minor releases.

  • 17 Dec 2025 · AI updates

    Gemini 3 Flash: "Pro-grade reasoning at Flash speed"

    Google released Gemini 3 Flash, positioned as frontier-level reasoning at Flash speed and lower cost; it became the default model in the Gemini app and in Search's AI Mode.

  • 20 Nov 2025 · AI updates

    Nano Banana Pro (Gemini 3 Pro Image) for developers

    Google DeepMind released Nano Banana Pro, an image generation and editing model built on Gemini 3 Pro, with more accurate text rendering, Google Search grounding, fine camera and lighting control, and up to 4K output.

  • 17 Oct 2025 · AI updates

    LangChain 1.0 released

    LangChain reached 1.0. The release notes list the final changes before it: new middleware (shell, file search, tool retry), an injectable ToolRuntime, state-schema options for create_agent, and Python 3.14 support.

  • 4 Sept 2025 · AI updates

    EmbeddingGemma: a 308M multilingual embedding model for devices

    Google released EmbeddingGemma, a 308M-parameter embedding model with a 2K context supporting 100+ languages; at release it was the top text-only multilingual model under 500M on MTEB.

  • 5 Aug 2025 · AI updates

    gpt-oss: OpenAI releases open-weight models under Apache 2.0

    OpenAI released gpt-oss-120b and gpt-oss-20b, mixture-of-experts reasoning models quantised with MXFP4 and licensed Apache 2.0; the large one fits on one H100 and the small one in 16GB.

  • 8 Jul 2025 · AI updates

    SmolLM3: an open 3B reasoner with the full recipe

    Hugging Face released SmolLM3, a 3B model trained on 11T tokens with dual think/no_think modes, six languages and 128K context, published with its complete training blueprint.

  • 26 Jun 2025 · AI updates

    Gemma 3n: on-device multimodal models with "effective" sizes

    Gemma 3n arrived across open-source tools; its E2B and E4B models have 5B and 8B real parameters but need only 2B- and 4B-class memory, and accept image, audio, video and text.

  • 15 May 2025 · AI updates

    vLLM 0.9: PyTorch 2.7, Blackwell kernels, large-scale inference

    vLLM 0.9.0 moved to PyTorch 2.7 and CUDA 12.8, added initial optimised kernels for NVIDIA Blackwell, and started data-parallel, expert-parallel and prefill/decode disaggregation support for large-scale inference.

  • 5 Apr 2025 · AI updates

    Llama 4 Scout and Maverick: Meta moves to mixture-of-experts

    Meta's Llama 4 introduces two natively multimodal MoE models with 17B active parameters each: Scout (~109B total, 16 experts) and Maverick (~400B total, 128 experts), trained on up to 40T tokens.

  • 12 Mar 2025 · AI updates

    Gemma 3: multimodal, 128K context, 140+ languages

    Google's Gemma 3 comes in 1B, 4B, 12B and 27B sizes; the 4B and up accept images, support 128K context and 140+ languages, and the 27B instruct model is reported to beat Gemini 1.5 Pro.

  • 4 Feb 2025 · AI updates

    Open-source DeepResearch in 24 hours

    The day after OpenAI's Deep Research launch, Hugging Face set out to reproduce its agentic framework openly in 24 hours, built on smolagents' CodeAgent and evaluated on GAIA.

  • 28 Jan 2025 · AI updates

    Open-R1: Hugging Face sets out to reproduce DeepSeek-R1 openly

    A week after DeepSeek-R1, Hugging Face launched Open-R1 to reconstruct its data and training pipeline in the open, since DeepSeek released weights and a report but no training code or datasets.

  • 31 Dec 2024 · AI updates

    smolagents: agents that write their actions as code

    Hugging Face launched smolagents, a minimal agent library whose main agent type writes its actions as Python code, plus a clear way to think about "agency" as a spectrum.

  • 25 Nov 2024 · AI updates

    Anthropic open-sources the Model Context Protocol (MCP)

    Anthropic open-sourced the Model Context Protocol, an open standard for connecting AI assistants to data sources and tools through MCP servers and clients, with a specification, SDKs, local server support in Claude Desktop and pre-built servers.

  • 17 Oct 2024 · AI updates

    PyTorch 2.5: cuDNN SDPA backend, FlexAttention, regional compilation

    PyTorch 2.5 added a cuDNN backend for scaled dot-product attention (speedups by default on H100-class GPUs), regional compilation to cut torch.compile cold-start time, and a FlexAttention prototype.

  • 25 Sept 2024 · AI updates

    Llama 3.2: vision models and small on-device models

    Meta's Llama 3.2 added vision models (11B and 90B) and small text-only models (1B and 3B) for on-device use, plus a vision-capable Llama Guard; ten open-weight models in total.

  • 7 Aug 2024 · AI updates

    LangGraph 0.2: pluggable checkpointers for durable agents

    LangGraph v0.2 split state persistence into dedicated checkpointer libraries (in-memory, SQLite and an open-sourced Postgres version) for session memory, error recovery and human-in-the-loop.

  • 23 Jul 2024 · AI updates

    Llama 3.1: 405B open weights, long context, tool calling

    Meta released Llama 3.1 in 8B, 70B and 405B sizes with multilingual support and long context, plus Llama Guard 3 and Prompt Guard; Hugging Face provided FP8, AWQ and GPTQ quantised versions.

  • 27 Jun 2024 · AI updates

    Gemma 2: 9B and 27B with sliding-window attention and distillation

    Google released Gemma 2 in 9B and 27B sizes, trained on about twice the data of Gemma 1 and using sliding-window attention, logit soft-capping, knowledge distillation and model merging.

  • 17 May 2024 · AI updates

    Transformers 4.41: GGUF support, Phi-3, PaliGemma

    Transformers v4.41 added Phi-3 (with 128K-context variants), the JetMoE mixture-of-experts model, Google's PaliGemma vision-language model and GGUF loading support.

  • 18 Apr 2024 · AI updates

    Meta releases Llama 3 (8B and 70B)

    Meta released Llama 3 in 8B and 70B sizes, base and instruct, plus Llama Guard 2 for safety classification, with an 8K context and day-one Hugging Face support.

  • 30 Mar 2024 · AI updates

    vLLM 0.4: automatic prefix caching

    vLLM 0.4.0 added automatic prefix caching (reusing the KV cache for shared prompt prefixes such as long system prompts), JSON mode in its OpenAI-compatible server, and new models including DBRX and Command R+.

  • 21 Feb 2024 · AI updates

    Google releases Gemma, open models based on Gemini

    Google released Gemma, open LLMs in 2B and 7B sizes (base and instruction-tuned) with an 8K context, built from Gemini research, with day-one Hugging Face support.

  • 30 Jan 2024 · AI updates

    PyTorch 2.2: FlashAttention-2 in SDPA, AOTInductor

    PyTorch 2.2 brought FlashAttention-2 into scaled_dot_product_attention for about 2× speedups, and introduced AOTInductor for compiling models ahead of time for non-Python deployment.

  • 11 Dec 2023 · AI updates

    Mixtral 8x7B arrives on Hugging Face

    Mistral released Mixtral 8x7B, a sparse mixture-of-experts LLM under Apache 2.0; Hugging Face integrated it into Transformers, TGI and Inference Endpoints on launch day.

  • 2 Nov 2023 · AI updates

    Transformers 4.35: safetensors by default, Distil-Whisper, Fuyu

    Hugging Face Transformers v4.35 made safetensors the default serialization format and added models including Distil-Whisper (6× faster than Whisper) and Adept's Fuyu-8B.

  • 4 Oct 2023 · AI updates

    PyTorch 2.1: dynamic shapes in torch.compile, distributed checkpointing

    PyTorch 2.1 added automatic dynamic-shape support to torch.compile, parallel distributed checkpointing, NumPy compilation, and a prototype of torch.export.

  • 13 Sept 2023 · AI updates

    Würstchen: diffusion in a 42× compressed latent space

    Würstchen is a text-to-image diffusion model whose text-conditional stage works in a latent space compressed 42× spatially, making training and inference much cheaper than Stable Diffusion.

  • 22 Aug 2023 · AI updates

    IDEFICS: an open reproduction of Flamingo

    Hugging Face released IDEFICS, an open visual language model in 9B and 80B sizes that reproduces DeepMind's unreleased Flamingo using only public data and models.
