About This Engineering Notebook

This site is my public engineering journal, starting on 4 August 2023 (my first day at PDEU) and updated continuously. It serves as both a structured learning handbook and an archival log across three primary tracks:

Study Guides Structured Technical Notes

Sequential, topic-by-topic curriculum notes across 15 engineering phases. Every code snippet is executed in a controlled Python/C++ environment prior to publishing, ensuring zero broken snippets and real outputs.

Paper Digests Frontier Research Breakdowns

Rigorous breakdowns of breakthrough papers from arXiv and Hugging Face Daily Papers, cataloging mathematical formulations, benchmarks, architectural diagrams, and verified limitations.

AI Updates Industry Releases

Concise, factual summaries of foundation model releases, technical reports, and major library milestones directly from official engineering blogs and model cards.

Integrity & Attribution Policy

Every single post cites its primary sources. Benchmarks, parameter counts, and dates are quoted strictly from verified public announcements or papers. All figures are either custom-rendered or reused under explicit Creative Commons (CC-BY) licensing with attribution.
