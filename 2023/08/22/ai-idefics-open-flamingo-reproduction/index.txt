1d:T519,<h2 id="what-happened">What happened</h2>
<p>Hugging Face released <strong>IDEFICS</strong> (Image-aware Decoder Enhanced à la Flamingo with Interleaved Cross-attentionS), an open-access visual language model. It reproduces <strong>Flamingo</strong>, a DeepMind model that was never publicly released.</p>
<h2 id="the-technical-details">The technical details</h2>
<ul>
<li>accepts <strong>arbitrary sequences of images and text</strong> and produces text: answering questions about images, describing them, and writing stories grounded in several images</li>
<li>built only on <strong>publicly available data and models</strong>: LLaMA v1 for language and OpenCLIP for vision</li>
<li>two variants, <strong>base</strong> and <strong>instructed</strong>, each at <strong>9B</strong> and <strong>80B</strong> parameters</li>
<li>the announcement says it's comparable in performance to the original Flamingo</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="why-it-matters">Why it matters</h2>
<p>The blog frames IDEFICS as a transparency effort: public data only, tools to explore the training data, a shared log of technical lessons and mistakes, and adversarial red-teaming before release.
