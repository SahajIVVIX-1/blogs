23:Tc76,<h2 id="what-happened">What happened</h2>
<p>On 25 September 2023 <strong>OpenAI</strong> announced voice and image capabilities for <strong>ChatGPT</strong>, rolling out to Plus and Enterprise users over the following two weeks. Users can hold a back-and-forth voice conversation, or show ChatGPT a picture of what they are talking about.</p>
<h2 id="the-technical-details">The technical details</h2>
<p>From the OpenAI post:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>voice</strong> on iOS and Android, opt-in under Settings → New Features, with a choice of five voices</li>
<li>a new <strong>text-to-speech model</strong> that generates human-like audio from text plus a few seconds of sample speech; the voices were created with professional voice actors</li>
<li><strong>Whisper</strong> transcribes the user's spoken words to text</li>
<li><strong>image input</strong> on all platforms, for example a photo of a landmark, of a fridge and pantry, or a circled math problem</li>
<li>the rollout to Plus and Enterprise users takes place over the next two weeks</li>
</ul>
