Paper summary: Large Language Models as Optimizers

OPRO describes an optimisation task in natural language and lets an LLM propose new solutions from a history of past solutions and scores, used mainly to optimise prompts.

  • The "optimizer" is a prompt containing earlier solutions and their scores; the LLM proposes the next candidates.
  • Prompts found this way beat human-written prompts by up to 8% on GSM8K and up to 50% on Big-Bench Hard, as reported.
