Mixtral 8x7B arrives on Hugging Face

Mistral released Mixtral 8x7B, a sparse mixture-of-experts LLM under Apache 2.0; Hugging Face integrated it into Transformers, TGI and Inference Endpoints on launch day.

  • Two of eight experts process each token, so Mixtral decodes at the speed of a ~12B dense model while holding far more parameters.
  • The weights are Apache 2.0, and the announcement reports it beats GPT-3.5 on many benchmarks.
