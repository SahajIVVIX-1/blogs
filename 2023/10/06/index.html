Paper summary: Kandinsky: an Improved Text-to-Image Synthesis with Image Prior and Latent Diffusion

Kandinsky combines a separately trained image prior, which maps CLIP text embeddings to CLIP image embeddings, with latent diffusion and a modified MoVQ autoencoder: 3.3B parameters, open weights.

  • A separate image prior turns a CLIP text embedding into a CLIP image embedding, which then conditions latent diffusion.
  • The authors report FID 8.03 on COCO-30K, the best among open-source models at the time.
