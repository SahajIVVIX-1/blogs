  • 2 Dec 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: From Code Foundation Models to Agents and Applications: A Comprehensive Survey and Practical Guide to Code Intelligence

    A large survey and practical guide to code LLMs, from data curation and pre-training through SFT, RL and coding agents, comparing general and code-specialised models and the gap between benchmarks and real development.

  • 25 Nov 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: General Agentic Memory Via Deep Research

    General Agentic Memory keeps a light memory plus the full history in a page-store, then uses a "researcher" agent to build the right context at request time, like just-in-time compilation for memory.

  • 13 Nov 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Lumine: An Open Recipe for Building Generalist Agents in 3D Open Worlds

    Lumine is an open recipe for a generalist game-playing agent built on a vision-language model: pixels in at 5 Hz, keyboard and mouse out at 30 Hz, completing a five-hour storyline and transferring zero-shot to other games.

  • 11 Aug 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: GLM-4.5: Agentic, Reasoning, and Coding (ARC) Foundation Models

    GLM-4.5 is an open MoE LLM (355B total, 32B active) with hybrid thinking and direct modes, trained on 23T tokens plus expert iteration and RL, reporting 70.1% TAU-Bench, 91.0% AIME 24 and 64.2% SWE-bench Verified.

  • 29 Jul 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Agentic Reinforced Policy Optimization

    ARPO is an RL algorithm for multi-turn tool-using agents that samples extra rollouts right after tool calls, where the model's token entropy spikes, and is reported to beat trajectory-level RL with half the tool calls.

  • 18 Jul 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: A Survey of Context Engineering for Large Language Models

    A survey of over 1,400 papers that defines "context engineering": retrieving, processing and managing everything put into an LLM's context, and how RAG, memory, tools and multi-agent systems build on it.

  • 8 Jul 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: MemOS: A Memory OS for AI System

    MemOS proposes a memory operating system for LLMs that manages plaintext, activation-based and parameter-level memory as one schedulable resource, built around "MemCube" units with provenance and versioning.

  • 13 May 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Seed1.5-VL Technical Report

    ByteDance's Seed1.5-VL pairs a 532M vision encoder with a 20B-active MoE LLM and reports state of the art on 38 of 60 public benchmarks, plus strong GUI-control and gameplay agent results.

  • 4 Apr 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Advances and Challenges in Foundation Agents: From Brain-Inspired Intelligence to Evolutionary, Collaborative, and Safe Systems

    A book-length survey that frames LLM agents through brain-inspired modules (memory, world model, reward, goals, emotion), then covers self-improvement, multi-agent systems and agent safety.

  • 7 Mar 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: START: Self-taught Reasoner with Tools

    START teaches a long chain-of-thought reasoning model to call a Python interpreter mid-reasoning, using inserted hints ("Wait, maybe using Python here is a good idea") and rejection-sampling fine-tuning.

  • 20 Feb 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Qwen2.5-VL Technical Report

    Qwen2.5-VL adds precise object localisation, robust document parsing and hour-long video understanding through a natively dynamic-resolution ViT and absolute time encoding; the 72B model is reported to match GPT-4o and Claude 3.5 Sonnet.

  • 27 Nov 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: ShowUI: One Vision-Language-Action Model for GUI Visual Agent

    ShowUI is a vision-language-action model for GUI agents that sees screenshots like a person, trims redundant screen tokens with a UI graph, and interleaves vision, language and actions.

  • 13 Aug 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: The AI Scientist: Towards Fully Automated Open-Ended Scientific Discovery

    The AI Scientist chains LLM steps that generate research ideas, write and run experiment code, write a full paper and review it with an automated reviewer, as a framework for fully automated research.

  • 25 Jul 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: OpenHands: An Open Platform for AI Software Developers as Generalist Agents

    OpenHands (formerly OpenDevin) is an open platform for agents that work like software developers, writing code, using a shell and browsing, with sandboxed execution and built-in benchmarks.

  • 23 Nov 2023 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: GAIA: a benchmark for General AI Assistants

    GAIA is a benchmark of 466 real-world questions that are easy for people but need reasoning, browsing, multimodality and tool use; humans scored 92% against 15% for GPT-4 with plugins.

  • 4 Aug 2023 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Learning to Model the World with Language

    Dynalang treats language as a signal for predicting the future: an agent learns a multimodal world model and acts from imagined rollouts.
