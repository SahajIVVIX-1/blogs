  • 15 Mar 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: MM1: Methods, Analysis & Insights from Multimodal LLM Pre-training

    Apple's MM1 ablates how to build multimodal LLMs and finds the data mix and image encoder (resolution, token count) matter a lot, while the vision–language connector design matters little.

  • 3 Dec 2023 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Merlin:Empowering Multimodal LLMs with Foresight Minds

    Merlin trains a multimodal LLM to predict object trajectories and then reason about future events, using two stages called Foresight Pre-Training and Foresight Instruction-Tuning.

  • 13 Nov 2023 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Florence-2: Advancing a Unified Representation for a Variety of Vision Tasks

    Florence-2 is a single sequence-to-sequence vision model that takes a text prompt as the task instruction and answers in text, covering captioning, detection, grounding and segmentation, trained on 5.4B annotations.

  • 29 Sept 2023 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Vision Transformers Need Registers

    ViTs repurpose some background patch tokens as internal scratch space, which produces high-norm artifacts; adding a few extra "register" tokens fixes this and smooths attention maps.

  • 9 Aug 2023 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: 3D Gaussian Splatting for Real-Time Radiance Field Rendering

    3D Gaussian Splatting represents a scene as a large set of optimised 3D Gaussians and renders them with a fast rasteriser, reaching real-time novel-view synthesis at 1080p.
