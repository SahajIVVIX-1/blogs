    Spectral-Spatial Interpretation explains multispectral image models with a full position × channel attribution matrix, using a local Shapley game at each position, so it shows which channels matter where.

    Paper summary: ABot-Earth 0.5: Generative 3D Earth Model

    ABot-Earth 0.5 generates large, seamless 3D Gaussian Splatting environments from satellite images alone, at under 10 minutes per square kilometre, viewable in web map engines and usable as a simulator for UAV navigation.

  • 4 Mar 2026 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Utonia: Toward One Encoder for All Point Clouds

    Utonia trains one self-supervised point-transformer encoder across very different point-cloud domains (remote sensing, LiDAR, indoor RGB-D, CAD, video-lifted points), and its features also help robot policies and spatial reasoning in VLMs.

  • 13 Jan 2026 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: BabyVision: Visual Reasoning Beyond Language

    BabyVision tests core visual abilities that don't depend on language, which young children handle easily; the best model tested (Gemini3-Pro-Preview) scores 49.7, behind 6-year-olds and far behind adults' 94.1.

  • 18 Aug 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: DINOv3

    DINOv3 scales self-supervised vision training and introduces Gram anchoring to stop dense feature maps from degrading during long training, producing a frozen backbone that beats specialised models across many tasks.

  • 22 Oct 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: FrugalNeRF: Fast Convergence for Extreme Few-shot Novel View Synthesis without Learned Priors

    FrugalNeRF trains NeRFs from very few views quickly, using weight-sharing multi-scale voxels and pseudo depth chosen by cross-scale reprojection error, without relying on learned priors.

  • 2 Aug 2024 · Paper summary

    SAM 2 extends Segment Anything to video with a streaming-memory Transformer and a data engine that built the largest video segmentation dataset to date.

    Paper summary: Vision language models are blind: Failing to translate detailed visual features into words

    BlindTest's seven simple visual tasks, such as whether two circles overlap or how many times two lines cross, trip up strong vision-language models: four state-of-the-art VLMs averaged 58.07%.

  • 14 Jun 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Depth Anything V2

    Depth Anything V2 trains a monocular depth teacher on synthetic images only, then teaches student models through large-scale pseudo-labelled real images, giving finer and more robust depth than V1.

  • 15 Mar 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: MM1: Methods, Analysis & Insights from Multimodal LLM Pre-training

    Apple's MM1 ablates how to build multimodal LLMs and finds the data mix and image encoder (resolution, token count) matter a lot, while the vision–language connector design matters little.

  • 3 Dec 2023 · Paper summary

    Merlin trains a multimodal LLM to predict object trajectories and then reason about future events, using two stages called Foresight Pre-Training and Foresight Instruction-Tuning.

  • 13 Nov 2023 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Florence-2: Advancing a Unified Representation for a Variety of Vision Tasks

    Florence-2 is a single sequence-to-sequence vision model that takes a text prompt as the task instruction and answers in text, covering captioning, detection, grounding and segmentation, trained on 5.4B annotations.

  • 29 Sept 2023 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Vision Transformers Need Registers

    ViTs repurpose some background patch tokens as internal scratch space, which produces high-norm artifacts; adding a few extra "register" tokens fixes this and smooths attention maps.

  • 9 Aug 2023 · Paper summary
