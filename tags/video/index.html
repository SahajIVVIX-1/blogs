  • 18 Oct 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Movie Gen: A Cast of Media Foundation Models

    Meta's Movie Gen is a set of foundation models for 1080p HD video with synchronised audio, plus personalisation and instruction-based editing; the largest video model is a 30B Transformer.

  • 5 Sept 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Loopy: Taming Audio-Driven Portrait Avatar with Long-Term Motion Dependency

    Loopy is a video diffusion model that animates a portrait from audio alone, using long-term motion modules instead of the spatial motion templates other methods add to keep movement stable.

  • 2 Aug 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: SAM 2: Segment Anything in Images and Videos

    SAM 2 extends Segment Anything to video with a streaming-memory Transformer and a data engine that built the largest video segmentation dataset to date.

  • 7 Jun 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: ShareGPT4Video: Improving Video Understanding and Generation with Better Captions

    ShareGPT4Video builds dense, precise video captions (40K with GPT-4V, then 4.8M with its own captioner) to improve both video understanding models and text-to-video generation.

  • 21 May 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: FIFO-Diffusion: Generating Infinite Videos from Text without Training

    FIFO-Diffusion generates arbitrarily long videos from a pretrained text-to-video diffusion model, with no extra training, by denoising a queue of frames at increasing noise levels.

  • 24 Jan 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Lumiere: A Space-Time Diffusion Model for Video Generation

    Lumiere generates the full duration of a video in one pass with a Space-Time U-Net that downsamples in both space and time, instead of generating keyframes and then filling in between.
