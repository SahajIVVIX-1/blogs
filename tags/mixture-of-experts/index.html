#mixture-of-experts

  • 15 Jan 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: MiniMax-01: Scaling Foundation Models with Lightning Attention

    MiniMax-01 combines lightning attention, a linear-attention variant, with a 32-expert MoE (456B total, 45.9B active) to train on 1M-token contexts and extrapolate to 4M at inference.

  • 1 Apr 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Jamba: A Hybrid Transformer-Mamba Language Model

    Jamba interleaves Transformer and Mamba layers and adds mixture-of-experts in some layers; the released configuration fits on one 80GB GPU and handles contexts up to 256K tokens.

  • 9 Jan 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Mixtral of Experts

    Mixtral 8x7B is a sparse mixture-of-experts model: each layer has 8 feed-forward experts and a router picks 2 per token, giving 47B total but 13B active parameters.
