    Paper summary: Spectral-Spatial Interpretation

    Spectral-Spatial Interpretation explains multispectral image models with a full position × channel attribution matrix, using a local Shapley game at each position, so it shows which channels matter where.

  • 11 Mar 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Feature-Level Insights into Artificial Text Detection with Sparse Autoencoders

    Using sparse-autoencoder features from Gemma-2-2B's residual stream to detect and explain AI-generated text, revealing a distinct LLM writing style, especially in information-dense domains.

  • 24 Feb 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: LLM-Microscope: Uncovering the Hidden Role of Punctuation in Context Memory of Transformers

    Tokens we treat as filler (punctuation, articles, stop words) carry a surprising share of an LLM's contextual memory; removing them hurts MMLU and long-context scores, and the paper releases a toolkit to measure this.

  • 27 Mar 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: The Unreasonable Ineffectiveness of the Deeper Layers

    Removing up to about half of the layers of some open LLMs, chosen by layer similarity and followed by a little QLoRA "healing", barely hurts question-answering performance.
