    Paper summary: Atria Dawn: The Dawn of Agentic Superintelligence

    Paper summary: Intern-S1-Pro: Scientific Multimodal Foundation Model at Trillion Scale

    Intern-S1-Pro is described as the first one-trillion-parameter scientific multimodal foundation model, covering over 100 specialised science tasks while staying top-tier among open models on general ability.

  • 17 Mar 2026 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: AI Can Learn Scientific Taste

    Reinforcement Learning from Community Feedback trains a "Scientific Judge" on signals like citations, and a "Scientific Thinker" that proposes research ideas; the judge generalises to future years and unseen fields.

  • 13 Aug 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: The AI Scientist: Towards Fully Automated Open-Ended Scientific Discovery

    The AI Scientist chains LLM steps that generate research ideas, write and run experiment code, write a full paper and review it with an automated reviewer, as a framework for fully automated research.
