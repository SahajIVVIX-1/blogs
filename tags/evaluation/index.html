    Paper summary: A.S.E: A Repository-Level Benchmark for Evaluating Security in AI-Generated Code

    A.S.E is a repository-level benchmark for the security of AI-generated code; it finds that current LLMs still struggle with secure coding, and that a bigger reasoning budget doesn't reliably help.

  • 10 Jul 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Vision language models are blind: Failing to translate detailed visual features into words

    BlindTest's seven simple visual tasks, such as whether two circles overlap or how many times two lines cross, trip up strong vision-language models: four state-of-the-art VLMs averaged 58.07%.

  • 3 May 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Prometheus 2: An Open Source Language Model Specialized in Evaluating Other Language Models

    Prometheus 2 is an open evaluator LM that does both direct scoring and pairwise ranking against user-defined criteria, built to track human and GPT-4 judgements more closely than earlier open judges.

  • 23 Nov 2023 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: GAIA: a benchmark for General AI Assistants

    GAIA is a benchmark of 466 real-world questions that are easy for people but need reasoning, browsing, multimodality and tool use; humans scored 92% against 15% for GPT-4 with plugins.

  • 12 Oct 2023 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Prometheus: Inducing Fine-grained Evaluation Capability in Language Models

    Prometheus is an open 13B evaluator LLM trained on GPT-4-generated feedback that scores long-form answers against a user-provided rubric, approaching GPT-4's agreement with human raters.
