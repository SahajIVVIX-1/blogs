#llm

  • 23 Apr 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Phi-3 Technical Report: A Highly Capable Language Model Locally on Your Phone

    phi-3-mini is a 3.8B model trained on 3.3T tokens of heavily filtered web and synthetic data, reported to reach 69% on MMLU while being small enough to run on a phone.

  • 16 Apr 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Learn Your Reference Model for Real Good Alignment

    Trust Region alignment (TR-DPO, TR-IPO, TR-KTO) updates the reference policy during offline alignment training, which reduces over-optimisation.

  • 11 Apr 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Leave No Context Behind: Efficient Infinite Context Transformers with Infini-attention

    Infini-attention adds a compressive memory to standard attention, combining local masked attention with long-term linear attention so Transformers can stream unbounded inputs with bounded memory.

  • 1 Apr 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Jamba: A Hybrid Transformer-Mamba Language Model

    Jamba interleaves Transformer and Mamba layers and adds mixture-of-experts in some layers; the released configuration fits on one 80GB GPU and handles contexts up to 256K tokens.

  • 27 Mar 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: The Unreasonable Ineffectiveness of the Deeper Layers

    Removing up to about half of the layers of some open LLMs, chosen by layer similarity and followed by a little QLoRA "healing", barely hurts question-answering performance.

  • 21 Mar 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: LlamaFactory: Unified Efficient Fine-Tuning of 100+ Language Models

    LlamaFactory is an open framework that brings many efficient fine-tuning methods together for 100+ LLMs, including a no-code web UI called LlamaBoard.

  • 15 Mar 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: MM1: Methods, Analysis & Insights from Multimodal LLM Pre-training

    Apple's MM1 ablates how to build multimodal LLMs and finds the data mix and image encoder (resolution, token count) matter a lot, while the vision–language connector design matters little.

  • 7 Mar 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: GaLore: Memory-Efficient LLM Training by Gradient Low-Rank Projection

    GaLore projects gradients into a low-rank subspace for the optimizer state while still updating all weights, cutting optimizer memory by up to 65.5% (82.5% in 8-bit) without LoRA's accuracy trade-off.

  • 28 Feb 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: The Era of 1-bit LLMs: All Large Language Models are in 1.58 Bits

    BitNet b1.58 restricts every weight to −1, 0 or +1 (about 1.58 bits) and reports matching full-precision LLMs of the same size and data on perplexity and end tasks, at much lower cost.

  • 22 Feb 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: LongRoPE: Extending LLM Context Window Beyond 2 Million Tokens

    LongRoPE extends a pretrained LLM's context window to 2048k tokens by searching for non-uniform RoPE interpolation, extending progressively, and readjusting for short contexts.

  • 16 Feb 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Chain-of-Thought Reasoning Without Prompting

    Chain-of-thought reasoning can appear without any prompting: branching on the top-k first tokens during decoding often reveals reasoning paths, and those paths come with higher answer confidence.

  • 6 Feb 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models

    DeepSeekMath 7B continues pre-training on 120B maths tokens mined from Common Crawl and introduces GRPO, a PPO variant without a value network, reaching 51.7% on the MATH benchmark.

  • 2 Feb 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: OLMo: Accelerating the Science of Language Models

    OLMo is a competitive language model released with its training data, training code and evaluation code, not just weights, so others can study how such models are built.

  • 18 Jan 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Self-Rewarding Language Models

    A model judges its own responses with LLM-as-a-Judge prompting and trains on those rewards with iterative DPO, so both its instruction following and its judging improve over three rounds.

  • 9 Jan 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Mixtral of Experts

    Mixtral 8x7B is a sparse mixture-of-experts model: each layer has 8 feed-forward experts and a router picks 2 per token, giving 47B total but 13B active parameters.

  • 27 Dec 2023 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: SOLAR 10.7B: Scaling Large Language Models with Simple yet Effective Depth Up-Scaling

    SOLAR 10.7B grows a smaller LLM by duplicating its layer stack, trimming the overlap and continuing pre-training ("depth up-scaling"), a simpler route than mixture-of-experts.

  • 20 Dec 2023 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: LLM in a flash: Efficient Large Language Model Inference with Limited Memory

    Apple researchers run LLMs larger than the available DRAM by keeping weights in flash and loading only what is needed, with windowing and row–column bundling designed around how flash memory reads data.

  • 12 Dec 2023 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: LLM360: Towards Fully Transparent Open-Source LLMs

    LLM360 argues for releasing everything behind an LLM (data, code, intermediate checkpoints, analyses) and starts with two fully open 7B models, Amber and CrystalCoder.

  • 4 Dec 2023 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Mamba: Linear-Time Sequence Modeling with Selective State Spaces

    Mamba makes state-space model parameters depend on the input, so the model can choose what to remember or forget, and pairs this with a hardware-aware scan: linear-time sequence modelling without attention.

  • 3 Dec 2023 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Merlin:Empowering Multimodal LLMs with Foresight Minds

    Merlin trains a multimodal LLM to predict object trajectories and then reason about future events, using two stages called Foresight Pre-Training and Foresight Instruction-Tuning.

  • 30 Oct 2023 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: CodeFusion: A Pre-trained Diffusion Model for Code Generation

    CodeFusion generates code with a diffusion model that iteratively denoises a whole program conditioned on the natural-language request, instead of writing tokens left to right.

  • 18 Oct 2023 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: BitNet: Scaling 1-bit Transformers for Large Language Models

    BitNet replaces nn.Linear with BitLinear and trains Transformers with 1-bit weights from scratch, reporting competitive perplexity with much lower memory and energy than FP16 or 8-bit baselines.

  • 12 Oct 2023 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Prometheus: Inducing Fine-grained Evaluation Capability in Language Models

    Prometheus is an open 13B evaluator LLM trained on GPT-4-generated feedback that scores long-form answers against a user-provided rubric, approaching GPT-4's agreement with human raters.

  • 22 Sept 2023 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: LongLoRA: Efficient Fine-tuning of Long-Context Large Language Models

    LongLoRA extends the context window of pretrained LLMs cheaply by fine-tuning with shifted sparse local attention plus a LoRA variant that also trains embeddings and norms.

  • 12 Sept 2023 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Textbooks Are All You Need II: phi-1.5 technical report

    phi-1.5 is a 1.3B-parameter model trained mostly on synthetic "textbook-quality" data, reported to match models about five times larger on common-sense reasoning.

  • 8 Sept 2023 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Large Language Models as Optimizers

    OPRO describes an optimisation task in natural language and lets an LLM propose new solutions from a history of past solutions and scores, used mainly to optimise prompts.

  • 14 Aug 2023 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Self-Alignment with Instruction Backtranslation

    Instruction backtranslation lets a language model label unlabelled web text with the instructions it answers, then pick the best pairs to fine-tune itself.
