    A large survey and practical guide to code LLMs, from data curation and pre-training through SFT, RL and coding agents, comparing general and code-specialised models and the gap between benchmarks and real development.

  • 30 Oct 2025 · Paper summary

    Ouro builds latent reasoning into pre-training with looped language models (iterating in latent space with a learned depth), and its 1.4B and 2.6B models are reported to match up to 12B models.

  • 20 Oct 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: A Theoretical Study on Bridging Internal Probability and Self-Consistency for LLM Reasoning

    A theoretical framework for sampling-based test-time scaling shows self-consistency has high estimation error and perplexity has high modelling error; the hybrid RPC method matches self-consistency with half the samples.

  • 14 Oct 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: QeRL: Beyond Efficiency -- Quantization-enhanced Reinforcement Learning for LLMs

    QeRL combines NVFP4 quantisation with LoRA for RL training of LLMs; beyond saving memory and speeding up rollouts, quantisation noise raises policy entropy and helps exploration. It enables RL on a 32B model with one H100.

  • 1 Oct 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: The Dragon Hatchling: The Missing Link between the Transformer and Models of the Brain

    The Dragon Hatchling (BDH) is a brain-inspired language-model architecture of locally interacting "neuron particles" with Hebbian working memory, reported to match GPT-2 at the same size and to be more interpretable.

  • 23 Sept 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Qwen3-Omni Technical Report

    Qwen3-Omni is a single model across text, image, audio and video that's reported to match same-sized single-modal Qwen models, with a Thinker–Talker MoE design and 234 ms theoretical first-packet speech latency.

  • 19 Sept 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: FlowRL: Matching Reward Distributions for LLM Reasoning

    FlowRL replaces reward maximisation with matching the whole reward distribution (via a learnable partition function and reverse KL), to keep diverse reasoning paths alive; it reports +10.0% over GRPO on maths.

  • 10 Sept 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Sharing is Caring: Efficient LM Post-Training with Collective RL Experience Sharing

    SAPO is a fully decentralised, asynchronous RL post-training method in which many heterogeneous nodes train their own models but share rollouts, reporting up to 94% higher cumulative reward in controlled tests.

  • 1 Sept 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: A.S.E: A Repository-Level Benchmark for Evaluating Security in AI-Generated Code

    A.S.E is a repository-level benchmark for the security of AI-generated code; it finds that current LLMs still struggle with secure coding, and that a bigger reasoning budget doesn't reliably help.

  • 11 Aug 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: GLM-4.5: Agentic, Reasoning, and Coding (ARC) Foundation Models

    GLM-4.5 is an open MoE LLM (355B total, 32B active) with hybrid thinking and direct modes, trained on 23T tokens plus expert iteration and RL, reporting 70.1% TAU-Bench, 91.0% AIME 24 and 64.2% SWE-bench Verified.

  • 25 Jul 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Group Sequence Policy Optimization

    Group Sequence Policy Optimization computes importance ratios and clipping at the level of whole sequences rather than tokens, and is reported to be more stable and efficient than GRPO, especially for MoE models.

  • 18 Jul 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: A Survey of Context Engineering for Large Language Models

    A survey of over 1,400 papers that defines "context engineering": retrieving, processing and managing everything put into an LLM's context, and how RAG, memory, tools and multi-agent systems build on it.

  • 8 Jul 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: MemOS: A Memory OS for AI System

  • 23 Jun 2025 · Paper summary

    Drag-and-Drop LLMs train a generator that maps a few unlabelled task prompts straight to LoRA weights, specialising an LLM for a new task in seconds without any gradient-based fine-tuning on it.

  • 17 Jun 2025 · Paper summary

    MiniMax-M1 is an open-weight hybrid-attention reasoning model (456B total, 45.9B active, 1M-token context) trained with large-scale RL and a new algorithm, CISPO, at a reported rental cost of $534,700.

  • 10 Jun 2025 · Paper summary

  • 4 Jun 2025 · Paper summary

    When a model fails a task, it writes a self-reflection and tries again; if the retry succeeds, the reflection tokens are rewarded, which trains better self-reflection with only binary feedback.

  • 19 May 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Qwen3 Technical Report

    Qwen3 merges a "thinking" mode for multi-step reasoning and a fast non-thinking mode in one model family (0.6B to 235B, dense and MoE), adds a thinking budget, and expands to 119 languages.

  • 7 May 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Absolute Zero: Reinforced Self-play Reasoning with Zero Data

    Absolute Zero trains a model to propose its own coding and maths-style tasks and then solve them, with a code executor checking both, so reasoning improves through self-play with no external training data.

  • 30 Apr 2025 · Paper summary

  • 21 Apr 2025 · Paper summary

  • 27 Mar 2025 · Paper summary

  • 11 Mar 2025 · Paper summary

  • 24 Feb 2025 · Paper summary
