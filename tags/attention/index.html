  • 17 Oct 2024 · AI updates

    PyTorch 2.5: cuDNN SDPA backend, FlexAttention, regional compilation

    PyTorch 2.5 added a cuDNN backend for scaled dot-product attention (speedups by default on H100-class GPUs), regional compilation to cut torch.compile cold-start time, and a FlexAttention prototype.

  • 8 Oct 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Differential Transformer

    Differential attention computes attention as the difference between two softmax maps, cancelling common-mode noise; Diff Transformer is reported to beat Transformers on scaling, long context, retrieval and hallucination.

  • 11 Apr 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Leave No Context Behind: Efficient Infinite Context Transformers with Infini-attention

    Infini-attention adds a compressive memory to standard attention, combining local masked attention with long-term linear attention so Transformers can stream unbounded inputs with bounded memory.

  • 30 Jan 2024 · AI updates

    PyTorch 2.2: FlashAttention-2 in SDPA, AOTInductor

    PyTorch 2.2 brought FlashAttention-2 into scaled_dot_product_attention for about 2× speedups, and introduced AOTInductor for compiling models ahead of time for non-Python deployment.
