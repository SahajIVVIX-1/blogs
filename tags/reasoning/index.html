    Paper summary: VGI-Bench: Probing Visual Intelligence in Video Generation Models

    VGI-Bench tests visual reasoning in video generation models with 27 tasks and 810 instances that require a valid process, not just a plausible final frame; the best model, Seedance 2.0, scores 51.0%.

  • 12 Aug 2026 · Study

    Agentic AI · ReAct

    ReAct interleaves reasoning ("Thought") with tool use ("Action") and results ("Observation"); the prompt format, a parser for model output, and a full trace with a scripted model.

  • 11 Aug 2026 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: BDH-CQ: In-Context Learning with Recurrent Latent Reasoning

    BDH-CQ combines in-context learning with recurrent latent reasoning in the Dragon Hatchling architecture; a 150M model reaches 29.5% pass@2 on ARC-AGI-1 at about $0.0007 per task, a new cost–accuracy frontier.

  • 29 Apr 2026 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Recursive Multi-Agent Systems

    RecursiveMAS treats a whole multi-agent system as one recursive computation in latent space, linking agents with a light RecursiveLink module and training them jointly; it reports +8.3% accuracy and 1.2–2.4× faster inference.

  • 24 Feb 2026 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: A Very Big Video Reasoning Suite

    VBVR is a video-reasoning dataset of 200 curated tasks and over a million clips, about 1,000× larger than earlier ones, with a rule-based benchmark used for an early study of how video reasoning scales.

  • 19 Feb 2026 · AI updates

    Gemini 3.1 Pro: upgraded core reasoning

    Google released Gemini 3.1 Pro in preview, the core model behind the previous week's Gemini 3 Deep Think update, rolling out across the Gemini API, Antigravity, Vertex AI, the Gemini app and NotebookLM.

  • 22 Jan 2026 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: A Survey of Agentic Reasoning for Large Language Models: Towards Recursively Self-Improving and Collective Agents

    A survey organising agentic reasoning into three layers (foundational single-agent skills, self-evolving agents, collective multi-agent reasoning), each split into in-context and post-training approaches.

  • 7 Nov 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Thinking with Video: Video Generation as a Promising Multimodal Reasoning Paradigm

    "Thinking with Video" uses a video generation model (Sora-2) as a reasoner, drawing its solution as video frames; on a new benchmark it rivals strong VLMs on visual puzzles and reaches 92% on MATH.

  • 30 Oct 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Scaling Latent Reasoning via Looped Language Models

    Ouro builds latent reasoning into pre-training with looped language models (iterating in latent space with a learned depth), and its 1.4B and 2.6B models are reported to match up to 12B models.

  • 20 Oct 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: A Theoretical Study on Bridging Internal Probability and Self-Consistency for LLM Reasoning

    A theoretical framework for sampling-based test-time scaling shows self-consistency has high estimation error and perplexity has high modelling error; the hybrid RPC method matches self-consistency with half the samples.

  • 8 Oct 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Less is More: Recursive Reasoning with Tiny Networks

    Tiny Recursive Model, a single 2-layer network with 7M parameters that repeatedly refines its answer, reports 45% on ARC-AGI-1 and 8% on ARC-AGI-2, above many large LLMs on these puzzles.

  • 19 Sept 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: FlowRL: Matching Reward Distributions for LLM Reasoning

    FlowRL replaces reward maximisation with matching the whole reward distribution (via a learnable partition function and reverse KL), to keep diverse reasoning paths alive; it reports +10.0% over GRPO on maths.

  • 26 Aug 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: InternVL3.5: Advancing Open-Source Multimodal Models in Versatility, Reasoning, and Efficiency

    InternVL3.5 adds Cascade RL (offline then online RL), a Visual Resolution Router and decoupled vision/language deployment, reporting up to +16% reasoning and 4.05× faster inference than InternVL3.

  • 11 Aug 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: GLM-4.5: Agentic, Reasoning, and Coding (ARC) Foundation Models

    GLM-4.5 is an open MoE LLM (355B total, 32B active) with hybrid thinking and direct modes, trained on 23T tokens plus expert iteration and RL, reporting 70.1% TAU-Bench, 91.0% AIME 24 and 64.2% SWE-bench Verified.

  • 2 Jul 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: GLM-4.5V and GLM-4.1V-Thinking: Towards Versatile Multimodal Reasoning with Scalable Reinforcement Learning

    GLM-4.1V-Thinking and GLM-4.5V use a reasoning-centric training framework with Reinforcement Learning with Curriculum Sampling (RLCS); GLM-4.5V is reported to lead open models of its size on nearly all of 42 benchmarks.

  • 17 Jun 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: MiniMax-M1: Scaling Test-Time Compute Efficiently with Lightning Attention

    MiniMax-M1 is an open-weight hybrid-attention reasoning model (456B total, 45.9B active, 1M-token context) trained with large-scale RL and a new algorithm, CISPO, at a reported rental cost of $534,700.

  • 19 May 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Qwen3 Technical Report

    Qwen3 merges a "thinking" mode for multi-step reasoning and a fast non-thinking mode in one model family (0.6B to 235B, dense and MoE), adds a thinking budget, and expands to 119 languages.

  • 7 May 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Absolute Zero: Reinforced Self-play Reasoning with Zero Data

    Absolute Zero trains a model to propose its own coding and maths-style tasks and then solve them, with a code executor checking both, so reasoning improves through self-play with no external training data.

  • 30 Apr 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Reinforcement Learning for Reasoning in Large Language Models with One Training Example

    RL with verifiable rewards on a single maths training example lifts Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B on MATH500 from 36.0% to 73.6%, matching training on a 1.2k-example dataset.

  • 21 Apr 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Does Reinforcement Learning Really Incentivize Reasoning Capacity in LLMs Beyond the Base Model?

    Measuring pass@k at large k, this study finds RL with verifiable rewards makes models better at k = 1 but doesn't expand what the base model can solve at all; distillation, by contrast, can.

  • 7 Mar 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: START: Self-taught Reasoner with Tools

    START teaches a long chain-of-thought reasoning model to call a Python interpreter mid-reasoning, using inserted hints ("Wait, maybe using Python here is a good idea") and rejection-sampling fine-tuning.

  • 10 Feb 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Scaling up Test-Time Compute with Latent Reasoning: A Recurrent Depth Approach

    A language model that "thinks" by looping a recurrent block more times at test time, reasoning in latent space instead of writing out more chain-of-thought tokens; a 3.5B proof of concept.

  • 28 Jan 2025 · AI updates

    A week after DeepSeek-R1, Hugging Face launched Open-R1 to reconstruct its data and training pipeline in the open, since DeepSeek released weights and a report but no training code or datasets.

  • 23 Jan 2025 · Paper summary

    DeepSeek-R1 shows that large-scale reinforcement learning, without human-labelled reasoning traces, can bring out advanced reasoning such as self-reflection and verification in a pretrained LLM.

  • 9 Jan 2025 · Paper summary

    rStar-Math gets small language models to strong maths reasoning with Monte Carlo Tree Search guided by a small process preference model, both improved through self-evolution without distillation from stronger models.

  • 22 Nov 2024 · Paper summary

    Mixed Preference Optimization and the automatically built MMPR dataset improve chain-of-thought reasoning in multimodal LLMs (InternVL2-8B and 76B) beyond what supervised fine-tuning alone gives.

  • 17 Nov 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: LLaVA-CoT: Let Vision Language Models Reason Step-by-Step

    LLaVA-CoT trains a vision-language model to reason in explicit stages (summary, visual interpretation, logical reasoning, conclusion) and adds a stage-wise retracing search at test time.

  • 16 Feb 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Chain-of-Thought Reasoning Without Prompting

    Chain-of-thought reasoning can appear without any prompting: branching on the top-k first tokens during decoding often reveals reasoning paths, and those paths come with higher answer confidence.

    DeepSeekMath 7B continues pre-training on 120B maths tokens mined from Common Crawl and introduces GRPO, a PPO variant without a value network, reaching 51.7% on the MATH benchmark.
