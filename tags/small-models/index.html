#small-models

  • 9 Jan 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: rStar-Math: Small LLMs Can Master Math Reasoning with Self-Evolved Deep Thinking

    rStar-Math gets small language models to strong maths reasoning with Monte Carlo Tree Search guided by a small process preference model, both improved through self-evolution without distillation from stronger models.

  • 27 Dec 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: YuLan-Mini: An Open Data-efficient Language Model

    YuLan-Mini is a 2.42B-parameter base model trained on 1.08T tokens that the authors report matches models trained on much more data, with full details of the data and training released.

  • 23 Apr 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Phi-3 Technical Report: A Highly Capable Language Model Locally on Your Phone

    phi-3-mini is a 3.8B model trained on 3.3T tokens of heavily filtered web and synthetic data, reported to reach 69% on MMLU while being small enough to run on a phone.

  • 12 Sept 2023 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Textbooks Are All You Need II: phi-1.5 technical report

    phi-1.5 is a 1.3B-parameter model trained mostly on synthetic "textbook-quality" data, reported to match models about five times larger on common-sense reasoning.
