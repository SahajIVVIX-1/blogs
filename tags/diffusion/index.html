  • 21 May 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: FIFO-Diffusion: Generating Infinite Videos from Text without Training

    FIFO-Diffusion generates arbitrarily long videos from a pretrained text-to-video diffusion model, with no extra training, by denoising a queue of frames at increasing noise levels.

  • 24 Jan 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Lumiere: A Space-Time Diffusion Model for Video Generation

    Lumiere generates the full duration of a video in one pass with a Space-Time U-Net that downsamples in both space and time, instead of generating keyframes and then filling in between.

  • 10 Nov 2023 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: LCM-LoRA: A Universal Stable-Diffusion Acceleration Module

    LCM-LoRA packages latent-consistency distillation as a LoRA that plugs into Stable Diffusion models and their fine-tunes to generate images in very few steps without extra training.

  • 30 Oct 2023 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: CodeFusion: A Pre-trained Diffusion Model for Code Generation

    CodeFusion generates code with a diffusion model that iteratively denoises a whole program conditioned on the natural-language request, instead of writing tokens left to right.

  • 6 Oct 2023 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: Kandinsky: an Improved Text-to-Image Synthesis with Image Prior and Latent Diffusion

    Kandinsky combines a separately trained image prior, which maps CLIP text embeddings to CLIP image embeddings, with latent diffusion and a modified MoVQ autoencoder: 3.3B parameters, open weights.

  • 13 Sept 2023 · AI updates

    Würstchen: diffusion in a 42× compressed latent space

    Würstchen is a text-to-image diffusion model whose text-conditional stage works in a latent space compressed 42× spatially, making training and inference much cheaper than Stable Diffusion.

  • 1 Sept 2023 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: MVDream: Multi-view Diffusion for 3D Generation

    MVDream trains a diffusion model to generate several consistent views of an object at once, then uses it as a 3D prior for text-to-3D, which reduces problems like the "two-faced horse".
