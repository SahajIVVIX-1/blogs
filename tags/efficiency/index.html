#efficiency

  • 14 Oct 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: QeRL: Beyond Efficiency -- Quantization-enhanced Reinforcement Learning for LLMs

    QeRL combines NVFP4 quantisation with LoRA for RL training of LLMs; beyond saving memory and speeding up rollouts, quantisation noise raises policy entropy and helps exploration. It enables RL on a 32B model with one H100.

  • 26 Aug 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: InternVL3.5: Advancing Open-Source Multimodal Models in Versatility, Reasoning, and Efficiency

    InternVL3.5 adds Cascade RL (offline then online RL), a Visual Resolution Router and decoupled vision/language deployment, reporting up to +16% reasoning and 4.05× faster inference than InternVL3.

  • 8 Apr 2025 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: SmolVLM: Redefining small and efficient multimodal models

    SmolVLM is a family of compact vision-language models designed for on-device inference; the 256M model uses under 1GB of GPU memory and is reported to beat the 300× larger Idefics-80B.

  • 7 Mar 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: GaLore: Memory-Efficient LLM Training by Gradient Low-Rank Projection

    GaLore projects gradients into a low-rank subspace for the optimizer state while still updating all weights, cutting optimizer memory by up to 65.5% (82.5% in 8-bit) without LoRA's accuracy trade-off.

  • 28 Feb 2024 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: The Era of 1-bit LLMs: All Large Language Models are in 1.58 Bits

    BitNet b1.58 restricts every weight to −1, 0 or +1 (about 1.58 bits) and reports matching full-precision LLMs of the same size and data on perplexity and end tasks, at much lower cost.

  • 20 Dec 2023 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: LLM in a flash: Efficient Large Language Model Inference with Limited Memory

    Apple researchers run LLMs larger than the available DRAM by keeping weights in flash and loading only what is needed, with windowing and row–column bundling designed around how flash memory reads data.

  • 10 Nov 2023 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: LCM-LoRA: A Universal Stable-Diffusion Acceleration Module

    LCM-LoRA packages latent-consistency distillation as a LoRA that plugs into Stable Diffusion models and their fine-tunes to generate images in very few steps without extra training.

  • 18 Oct 2023 · Paper summary

    Paper summary: BitNet: Scaling 1-bit Transformers for Large Language Models

    BitNet replaces nn.Linear with BitLinear and trains Transformers with 1-bit weights from scratch, reporting competitive perplexity with much lower memory and energy than FP16 or 8-bit baselines.

  • 13 Sept 2023 · AI updates

    Würstchen: diffusion in a 42× compressed latent space

    Würstchen is a text-to-image diffusion model whose text-conditional stage works in a latent space compressed 42× spatially, making training and inference much cheaper than Stable Diffusion.
