This is my first **unsupervised** algorithm: there are no labels, just structure to find.

**k-means**:
1. Pick k starting centres (scikit-learn uses the smarter *k-means++* initialisation).
2. **Assign** every point to its nearest centre.
3. **Update** each centre to the mean of its assigned points.
4. Repeat until nothing changes.

It minimises **inertia**, the within-cluster sum of squared distances. Inertia always falls as k grows, so to choose k, look for an **elbow** or use the **silhouette score** (−1 to 1, higher is better).

```python run
from sklearn.datasets import make_blobs
from sklearn.cluster import KMeans
from sklearn.metrics import silhouette_score, adjusted_rand_score

X, y_true = make_blobs(n_samples=600, centers=4, cluster_std=0.9, random_state=7)

for k in range(2, 8):
    km = KMeans(n_clusters=k, n_init=10, random_state=0).fit(X)
    print(f"k={k}: inertia {km.inertia_:8.1f} | silhouette {silhouette_score(X, km.labels_):.3f}")

best = KMeans(n_clusters=4, n_init=10, random_state=0).fit(X)
print("agreement with true blobs (ARI):", round(adjusted_rand_score(y_true, best.labels_), 3))
print("centres:\n", best.cluster_centers_.round(2))
```
